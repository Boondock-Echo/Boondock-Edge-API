# Important Safety Notice

## Not an emergency service

This software is not a Public Safety Answering Point (PSAP), 911 service, emergency communications center, emergency dispatch system, computer-aided dispatch system, emergency notification system, or life-safety system.

Do not use or rely on this software:

- to contact or summon police, fire, emergency medical, rescue, or other emergency services;
- as a substitute for 911 or another authorized emergency service;
- as the primary or required means of monitoring emergency radio traffic;
- to make dispatch, evacuation, incident-command, medical, fireground, rescue, or other safety-critical decisions;
- to protect life, health, essential services, or property; or
- in any situation in which delay, interruption, incorrect output, missed audio, data loss, or system failure could cause or contribute to injury, death, delayed emergency response, or property damage.

## Software limitations

Depending on configuration and external services, the software may experience:

- missed, delayed, duplicated, incomplete, or corrupted audio;
- inaccurate transcription, translation, classification, or keyword detection;
- network, power, radio, storage, hardware, cloud-service, or third-party failures;
- incorrect device configuration or incompatible equipment;
- software defects, security incidents, service interruptions, or data loss; and
- alerts that are late, incorrect, incomplete, or never delivered.

Radio reception does not guarantee complete or authoritative incident information. A displayed transcription or alert must not be treated as an official instruction or verified emergency communication.

## Supplemental use only

The software may be used only as a supplemental informational or hobby tool when nobody relies on it for emergency notification, dispatch, response, life safety, or protection of property. Maintain independent, authorized, and appropriately certified systems, procedures, personnel, and communication paths for operational and emergency purposes.

If an emergency may be occurring, contact the appropriate authorized emergency service using an approved communication method. In the United States, call or text 911 where available.
